SpaSE-UNet3D: Sensor-Driven Wildfire Detection and Progression Prediction from VIIRS Multispectral Imagery

      Accepted in MDPI Sensors journal
    

    
MIT licensed


    

      
      
      
    

    
Schematic, not model output: a fire front advancing across a VIIRS tile — burn scar behind it, active front on the edge, and in cyan the perimeter the model is asked to place for the next acquisition.


    

      PAPER
      
        Nikolaos Mavros · Dimitrios Katsaros
        Accepted 10 August 2026, manuscript sensors-4426418. In press: no DOI or page numbers until it appears. Certificate of acceptance (PDF)
      
    


    
Wildfire remote sensing on the TS-SatFire benchmark — VIIRS multispectral stacks from Suomi-NPP and NOAA-20, scored on detecting the fire that is burning now and predicting where its front goes next. Two contributions:


    

      

        
The architecture

        
A 3D U-Net whose convolutions are spatial-only (1, 3, 3): it refuses to mix across days, because the observation windows are too short to pay for it, and squeeze-and-excitation reweights the VIIRS bands instead. F1 0.8549 on active fire, matching the strongest baselines on their own terms with a third of their temporal context at 2.72× fewer parameters.

      

      

        
The audit

        
Many fires in the benchmark carry no ground truth at all. 18 training fires and 2 test fires are excluded for active fire — and those two cannot be scored by any model, not just this one. Training on audited data raised recall from ~0.80 to 0.86, which makes the label quality the finding rather than the model.

      

    


    
Reproduces on a Kaggle T4 inside the 12-hour session limit. github.com/mavroul1s/DeepFire-Forecaster →

  


  

    
NEOW / neo_tracker


    
      
      
      
      
        ▸ Launch tracker
        Live orbital viewer · runs in this browser
      
    

    

      

        
A watch post for the traffic around Earth. Pick a date range and see every near-Earth object NASA is tracking in it — how close it passes, how big it is, how well its orbit is known — placed in a solar system you can fly around.

        
Nothing is animated: eight planets, fifteen moons and every tracked asteroid are propagated from published elements through a Kepler solver. Lock onto a contact for its telemetry, or sort the registry by miss distance.

        
It ships on the shared DEMO_KEY, which is rate-limited. Paste a free key from api.nasa.gov into the data link panel for live close-approach data over any dates, with the hazard and uncertainty codes as NASA publishes them.

        
Publishes its own scale factors rather than faking scale, and corrects the "potentially hazardous means close now" misconception. Bilingual English / Ελληνικά. One HTML file, three.js only.

      

      

        
Checked against independent ground truth

        
    
          
  • Earth perihelion 0.98329 AU, aphelion 1.01671 AU
    • 
          
  • Equinox and solstice solar longitudes within 0.4°
    • 
          
  • Kepler solver converges to machine precision through e = 0.99
    • 
          
  • Earth computed twice by unrelated routes, agreeing to 44,000 km and 59 arcseconds
    • 
        

        

          ▸Open tracker
          ▸Source
        

      

    

  


  

    
Sector 07 — Grid Expansion


    
      
      
      
      
        ▸ Enter the grid
        Isometric defence grid · runs in this browser
      
    

    

      

        
Isometric cyberpunk city-builder and defence game. You hold one megacity sector: build nodes onto empty pads, let them link into a network, and purge intrusions before their timers run out — six rings deep around a central spire.

        
The whole thing is one HTML file. Geometry, icons and audio are generated at runtime rather than loaded, and every balance number — costs, yields, steal rates, drone stats — sits in a single object at the top of the file.

      

      

        
What is actually in there

        
    
          
  • 117 plots across six rings, claimed outward from the core
    • 
          
  • Eight node classes over five tiers; each link pays +8%, an isolated node loses 25%
    • 
          
  • Six breach classes unlock as threat climbs SECURE → OVERRUN
    • 
          
  • Every building merges to ~3 draw calls, which is what makes tier-5 towers affordable
    • 
          
  • 1,400 citizens, 260 vehicles and ~1,100 data packets drawn as points
    • 
          
  • Audio is synthesised through the Web Audio API — no sound files
    •

          ▸Source
        

      

    

  


  


    

      
neuron-death

      
PyTorch/335 runs/MIT

      
Do the methods that revive dormant neurons work by reviving dead ones? Analysis-only study of continual learning, with the analysis plan frozen before any data existed. Its control is the missing one: at every recycling event, recycle the same number of units chosen at random. Random selection recovers most of the benefit, and units left alone come back by themselves.

      
      

        Random control 88–92% of ReDo's gain
        Dead units revive 21.7% within one task
        Status experiments done, writing
        Open repository

NCF Reproduction

      
PyTorch/ECE480

      
Reproduction of Neural Collaborative Filtering (He et al., WWW 2017) on MovieLens-100K. Every experiment repeated ten times with independent seeds, all results reported as mean ± standard deviation. MLP depth and GMF+MLP pretraining show no mean benefit at this dataset size, contradicting the paper's MovieLens-1M finding.

      
      

        NeuMF best HR@10 0.649 ± 0.011
        KD student HR@10 0.655 ± 0.010
        FitNets-style 0.582
        Open repository
      

    


    

      
vit-gradient-flow

      
Julia/ECE452

      
A Vision Transformer built from scratch in Julia — attention, initialisation and training loop — in order to take matrix calculus to it. The Jacobian of the self-attention mechanism is derived by hand with matrix differential rules, then checked numerically against ForwardDiff.jl to prove the derivation right. With that in hand, the experiment: does orthogonal initialisation (WᵀW = I) keep gradients better conditioned through attention than Xavier/Glorot? Measured on Jacobian condition numbers and feature saliency, with MNIST fed in as sequential patches.

      
      

        Commits 52
        Verification numerical vs analytic
        Open repository
